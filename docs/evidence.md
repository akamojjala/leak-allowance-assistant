# Leak allowance: evidence

## What to submit
Every application needs a plumber's invoice showing the repair date and where the leak was, and a meter reading taken after the repair. Photos of the repaired pipework help if you have them.

## Uploading scanned documents
Upload scanned invoices and forms as PDF or JPG files of up to 10 MB each. Contoso Water reads uploaded documents automatically and adds the details to your application.

## Missing evidence
If evidence is missing, we ask for it by email. If we do not receive it within 28 days, the application is closed and you need to apply again.
