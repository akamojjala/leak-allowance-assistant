# Leak allowance: eligibility

## Leaks we cover
We cover leaks on the customer's underground supply pipe, which is the pipe between the property boundary and the building, and leaks on internal pipework inside the property.

## Leaks we do not cover
We do not cover leaks from appliances such as washing machines and dishwashers, dripping taps, leaking toilet cisterns, swimming pools or outdoor hoses. We also do not cover water lost through deliberate use or negligence.

## One allowance every 24 months
A property can receive one leak allowance in any 24-month period. A second leak within 24 months of a previous allowance is not eligible.
