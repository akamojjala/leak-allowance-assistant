# Leak allowance: business customers

## Who counts as a business customer
Any non-household account, including shops, offices, farms and schools.

## What is different for businesses
Business customers follow the same rules as households, except that the maximum allowance is £10,000 and the plumber's invoice must show the business account number.

## Large leaks
If a business allowance would be more than £5,000, a Contoso Water engineer may visit to confirm the repair before we pay.
