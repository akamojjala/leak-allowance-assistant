# Leak allowance: how we calculate it

## The formula
We compare your daily water use during the leak with your normal daily use before the leak. The excess, in cubic metres, is multiplied by our charges. One cubic metre is 1,000 litres.

## Charges we refund
We refund the water charge of £2.10 per cubic metre and the wastewater charge of £2.40 per cubic metre, a total of £4.50 per cubic metre, because leaked water does not enter the sewer.

## Maximum allowance
The maximum allowance is £3,000 per application for household customers and £10,000 per application for business customers.

## Worked example
A household normally uses 300 litres a day. During a 60-day leak it used 1,300 litres a day. The excess is 1,000 litres a day for 60 days, which is 60 cubic metres. The allowance is 60 × £4.50 = £270.
