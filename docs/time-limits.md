# Leak allowance: time limits

## Repairing the leak
The leak must be repaired within 30 days of being found. If the repair takes longer than 30 days, the application is not eligible unless Contoso Water caused the delay.

## Applying
Apply within 6 months of the repair date. Applications received more than 6 months after the repair are not accepted.

## How far back we go
We cover the leak period up to a maximum of 180 days before the repair date.
