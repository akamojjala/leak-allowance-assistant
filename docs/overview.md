# Leak allowance: overview

## What a leak allowance is
A leak allowance is a one-off reduction to a metered customer's bill when a leak on the customer's own pipework pushed their water use above normal. It refunds the charges for the water that leaked. It does not pay for the repair.

## Who can apply
Any metered Contoso Water customer, household or business, who has found and repaired a leak on pipework they are responsible for.

## How to apply
Apply online on the Contoso Water website by completing the leak allowance form and uploading your evidence. Most complete applications are decided within 10 working days.
