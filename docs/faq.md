# Leak allowance: frequently asked questions

## Does the allowance pay for the repair?
No. The allowance refunds the charges for the water that leaked. The cost of the repair is the customer's responsibility.

## Can a landlord apply for a tenant?
The account holder must apply. If the tenant is the account holder, the tenant applies.

## How will I receive the allowance?
As a credit on your next bill. If your account is closed, we pay by bank transfer.

## What if I do not have a meter?
Unmetered customers pay a fixed charge, so a leak does not change their bill and no allowance is needed.
